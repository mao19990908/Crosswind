```text
(vehicle OR automobile OR passenger car) AND
(crosswind gust OR transient crosswind OR lateral wind disturbance) AND
(yaw response OR side force OR yawing moment)
```

```text
(road vehicle) AND (gust response OR crosswind sensitivity) AND
(wind tunnel OR moving model OR full-scale OR CFD OR multibody)
```

```text

AND (crosswind OR gust)
```
