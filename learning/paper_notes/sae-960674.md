## Full-text Pass 2 update (2026-09-25)

The earlier `NEEDS_FULLTEXT` line is superseded: all nine pages and Figs. 3–8 were read. This is a moving-model, fixed-tunnel-crossflow experiment on three simple shapes in smooth, **non-skewed** flow. About 12 m/s model speed is reported for the plotted runs (p. 5). The 15° shorthand needs three separate statements: front-lift differences begin near 15°; side force stays similar until about 30°; yaw-moment **peak** rather than mean dynamic value separates above 15° (pp. 7–8). Fig. 8 and paper text support these distinctions. The study uses internal steady/dynamic comparison, not an independent transient-load or road-motion validation.

The track signal is position aligned and zero-yaw subtracted, with a nine-repeat coefficient standard deviation table (p. 6 Fig. 6). This improves interpretation of the measured loads but does not remove systematic signal artifacts or give full dynamic balance transfer characteristics. Sign convention is mirrored from MIRA and moments are translated to ground plane; drag is absent, so pitch moment at that reference is unavailable (p. 5). Pass 3 remains open for raw-signal filtering/axis reproduction and an explicit cross-paper comparison with Ryan 1998 under matched force definitions.
