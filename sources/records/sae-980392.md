## Physical problem and method
- Generic hatchback at 30° relative incidence in Durham facility; stationary model with secondary crosswind jet separated from main jet by moving belt/aperture.
- Surface-pressure array used to derive transient force and moment coefficients.
- Scale, Reynolds number, exact gust dimensions/timing, coordinate and normalization: not reported in abstract.

## Results / validation / evidence
- Transient side and lift forces exceed corresponding steady values by 10%–20% under the reported condition.
- Evidence: SAE abstract; no universal generalization beyond this configuration.

## Limitations / relevance / follow-up
- Limitation: one reported relative incidence and abstract-only.
- Relevance: quantitative evidence of non-quasi-steady peak amplification.
- Follow-up: extract yaw moment behavior, force normalization, gust edge and uncertainty.
