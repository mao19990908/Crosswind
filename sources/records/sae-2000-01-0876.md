## Full-text Pass 2 review (2026-09-25)

- `verification_status=full_text_verified`; earlier abstract-only statements are superseded. All 10 PDF pages read and Figs.4–17 inspected. This is the Cranfield moving-model crossflow track, not the Durham fixed-model jet: 17.5 m track across a 2.4 × 1.2 m tunnel, 5-component balance plus five differential pressure transducers; model speed about 13 m/s; Reynolds number about 3.5×10^5 based on length (article pp.2–3).
- Three simple shapes: 480×200 mm flat plate, 480×200×200 mm sharp box, and box with 20 mm edge radii, each 30 mm ground clearance. Pressure taps are on an approximately 50 mm grid, and integration therefore has limited spatial accuracy (p.3). Pressure acquisition had a frequency-response correction and a 30 Hz low-pass Hamming filter; authors acknowledge removal of higher-frequency aerodynamic response and likely filter-generated oscillations in side-force traces (pp.2,4).
- Flat plate traversed approximately five body lengths of gust and, especially at larger yaw, did not reach steady pressure before exit. It showed small yaw-moment transients relative to high baseline (pp.3–4, Figs.4–7). For the sharp box, yaw-moment peak occurs about one length after entry; peak/mean ≈2.5 at 25° yaw; the exit trough and entry peak are largely leeward-face pressure effects. Direct balance and coarse pressure integration agree in selected ≈10°/20° cases, an internal consistency check (pp.4–5,9, Figs.8–12,17).
- Radiused box has higher steady yaw moment but entry peak near the sharp-box level, peak/mean ≈1.5; the exit minimum is about 40% less extreme than the sharp box under compared conditions because the pressure action shifts toward mid-axis (pp.5–7, Figs.13–16). These ratios have different geometry and mean denominator, and must not be pooled into one vehicle-wide overshoot number.
- The paper itself flags competing gust-development lengths from Docton and Kobayashi; its own five-length profile failed steady development for some yaw conditions (p.3). Article p.8 references directly list `sae-960674` (ref3) and `sae-980392` (ref7). Body p.2 calls Macklin “[4]” although reference 4 is Cairns' thesis; treat this as a numbering error and verify direct citations from the explicit bibliography, not that in-text marker.
- No free vehicle motion or driver loop; only internal balance/pressure agreement, not independent facility wind-field or vehicle response validation.
