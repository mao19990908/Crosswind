## Physical problem and method
- Operational Volkswagen Golf 7 at BMW Aschheim side-wind facility, relative yaw β=22°–45°.
- Incoming flow measured with eleven five-hole dynamic-pressure probes; 188 time-resolved surface-pressure locations measured separately.
- Sampling, filtering, exact speeds, coordinates and normalization: not reported in abstract.

## Results / validation / evidence
- Leeward separated flow develops more slowly than windward flow; vehicle locally enters wind progressively.
- Pressure-integrated forces/moments show overshoot, undershoot and temporal lag relative to steady crosswind.
- Validation: Level A incoming flow plus Level B full-scale pressure/aerodynamic integration; no Level C vehicle motion.

## Limitations / relevance / follow-up
- Limitation: aerodynamic characterization alone does not validate vehicle stability.
- Relevance: high-value full-scale bridge for CFD and reduced-scale validation.
- Follow-up: full-text parameter extraction and uncertainty budget.
