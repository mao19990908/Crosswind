## Physical problem and method
- Generic 3-D vehicle in Durham sharp-edged finite-length gust facility.
- About 7,000 gusts per survey; each wake point phase-averaged over 20 gusts.
- Detailed scales, sampling, coordinate and normalization: not reported in abstract.

## Results / validation / evidence
- Developed wake structure and total-pressure loss become comparable with quasi-steady flow, but developing flow contains features absent from quasi-steady measurements.
- Evidence: SAE abstract.
