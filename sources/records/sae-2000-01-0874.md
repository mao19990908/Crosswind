## Full-text review and evidence correction (2026-09-25)

- `verification_status=full_text_verified`; previous abstract-only caveats are superseded. All 11 PDF pages were read, including the figure pages. Article pp.2–5, Figs.1–10, and references p.9 were checked.
- Facility is a fixed generic Durham model with computer-controlled sequential crosswind shutters, explicitly described through 1999 SAE 1999-01-0808. Its approximately 5 m / 10-model-length / 22° event differs from the 1998 Durham 1.5 m / three-length / 30° pressure experiment. Do not assume hardware, waveform or force coefficients are identical. 225 mid-height empty-section points at 100 mm spacing and 348 nodes per wake plane at 20 mm spacing were acquired with a calibrated five-hole probe at 800 Hz/channel, 250 Hz anti-alias filter, 20-gust phase averages; about 7000 gusts per plane (p.2).
- Empty-section Fig.2/5/6 shows about 10° pre-gust yaw undershoot and 12° leading-edge overshoot relative to nominal 22°. A local total-pressure coefficient reached about 1.7 versus 1.16 theoretical nominal maximum; the authors could not explain its cause and acknowledged bias in local surface-pressure normalization. They cite an auxiliary two-dimensional body survey for little net side/yaw contribution, not an independent transient force validation (pp.3–4).
- Three wake planes were surveyed, with the furthest at half a model length aft of the trailing edge displayed. Steady wake comparators used 0°, 7°, 14° and 22° yaw. Transient vortices and total-pressure deficits evolve toward steady-like structures (pp.3–5, Figs.7–10). Authors warn that a single wake plane at one time contains contributions advected from different vehicle positions, so its integral is not instantaneous whole-body force (p.5). Level A input and wake-field evidence only; no integrated force or vehicle-motion validation.
- The 20-event phase mean conceals event-level variability by construction, but no distribution or uncertainty is reported. Reference 1 directly cites `sae-960674`; reference 2 identifies the 1999 improved facility paper. `sae-980392` is not directly cited here; any facility link must be qualified as a Durham lineage with changed gust conditions.
