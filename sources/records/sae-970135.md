## Full-text review and evidence correction (2026-09-25)

- `verification_status=full_text_verified`; the preceding abstract-only limitations are superseded. All 14 PDF pages were read; article pp.31–34 and Figs.3–9 were checked against the PDF. This is a fixed-tunnel crosswind crossed by a moving squareback model, with no driver or free vehicle motion.
- Facility: 2.4 × 1.2 m tunnel, typically 1/5-scale vehicle, 12–18 m/s traverse, internal five-component balance and wind-off track subtraction (article p.31). Four rectangular-grid turbulence cases had nominal freestream intensity 1–3.5%, rising substantially near the floor. The authors made no atmospheric turbulence scaling attempt (pp.31–32, Figs.3–5).
- Five imposed vertical velocity profiles included roughness-board cases. These changed both mean shear and turbulence intensity/scale, so the larger front/rear lift and yaw-moment changes at 0–50° yaw cannot be assigned to shear alone. Side force and roll changed less in these cases (p.33, Figs.6–7). No specific atmospheric boundary layer was reproduced.
- For dynamic runs, changing the axial reference speed changes both resolved yaw and dynamic pressure; for static runs it changes pressure normalization only (p.33). Seven candidate reference definitions were tested, including height means and point values. None collapsed every coefficient; height-mean improved lift correlation, within this one geometry (pp.33–34, Figs.8–9).
- The abstract inference that turbulence is generally unimportant is too broad. The result is conditional on these four grids, squareback model, tunnel and coefficient definitions; a horizontal-bar case differed. Authors themselves caution that roughness-board shear effects are confounded with turbulence (pp.32–33).
- Internal repeatability reuses nine-run data from SAE 960674; no independent transient load or vehicle-motion reference here. `sae-960674` appears as direct reference 1 (p.34). Learning target Pass 2 is complete; re-open only for independent profile digitization or new contradictory evidence.
