## Results / validation / evidence
- Repository abstract links crosswinds to degraded driving stability and correlates lateral acceleration/yaw velocity with driver assessment; simulator also identifies path curvature as significant.
- Suggests forward CG and reduced yaw moment/rearward aerodynamic center reduce lateral response.
- Separate high-speed wake findings must not be conflated with transient gust findings.
