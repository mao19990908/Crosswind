## Physical problem and method
- Model-scale 3/4 open-jet closed-loop return tunnel with three subsystems evaluated separately and together.
- Generates yaw standard deviations 2.1°–8.0°, gusts >15% mean speed, and turbulence from a few percent passive to >20% active; frequency sweeps cover low-frequency spectra.
- Spatial coherence, exact integral scales, sampling and uncertainty: not reported in abstract.

## Results / validation / evidence
- Facility can combine yaw-distribution control, transient events and broadband turbulence with an inertial subrange.
- Validation is Level A device/flow characterization only; no vehicle aerodynamic or motion result in abstract.

## Limitations / relevance / follow-up
- Limitation: “on-road-like” spectral coverage does not by itself prove real-road equivalence.
- Relevance: current environment-reconstruction method node.
- Follow-up: extract calibration, repeatability, spatial uniformity/coherence and target road datasets.
