## Physical problem and method
- One-box vehicle entering crosswind and passing another vehicle.
- Sliding mesh with discontinuous interface, ALE method, k–ε model, third-order upwind convection.
- Abstract mentions about one million grid points for conventional methods and about 100 h on an engineering workstation for presented results; exact mesh/time step/CFL not reported.
