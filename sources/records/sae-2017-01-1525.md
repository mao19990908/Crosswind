## Full-text Pass 2 and Pass 3 boundary review (2026-09-25)

- `verification_status=full_text_verified`; abstract-only claims above are superseded. All nine PDF pages read; article pp.662–670, Figs.5–27 checked. The model uses coarse-mesh wall-modeled LES / very-large-eddy simulation with Smagorinsky `Cs=0.15`, ALE moving boundary, about 40 million cells and aerodynamic step `4×10^-5 s`; it exchanges CFD loads and CarSIM 6DoF state via TCP/IP. For 3.5 s there are 87,500 aerodynamic and 3,500 vehicle steps (pp.663–665). Coordinate frames differ between CFD and CarSIM and are mapped at the interface (Fig.5).
- Simulated hatchback length 4.25 m, wheelbase 2.70 m, frontal area 2.23 m², vehicle speed 33.3 m/s; side wind 12.1 m/s, nominal 20° and about 1.2 s. The step-like computational input has sinusoidal rise to avoid oscillation and is a simplification of the measured profile, not exact replay (pp.664–665, Fig.6). Tyre rotation omitted; far-floor free slip and near-floor wall law are further simplifications.
- **Validation is split by control mode.** The quantitative yaw-rate/lateral-acceleration comparison (Figs.10–12) is from **fixed steering**, an open-loop full-scale road test, not validation of the driver model. Wind-tunnel steady 20° side-force/yaw measurements have absolute mismatch but preserve broad aero-part trend (Fig.9). In the separate active-driver application, three road runs per specification show initial corrective steering reduction about 9% and exit correction about 17% in Fig.26, but model/measurement magnitudes diverge and event-to-event spread is visible (p.669). No independent driver-parameter validation or uncertainty interval reported.
- Driver model uses 50 m preview lateral path error plus lateral acceleration and first-order delay (p.667, Figs.14–15). Aero parts scarcely lower *steady* 20° yaw moment and increase steady side force slightly (Fig.13), yet reduce transient side-force fluctuation and corrective steering in this case (Figs.16–24). The claimed C-pillar spoiler flow-separation mechanism is supported by simulated flow/pressure visualization, not an isolated component experiment; other aero parts were added together.
- Full coupled sign/coordinate and controller gain reproduction remains Pass 3 pending; this read verifies the documented architecture and conditional validation but does not replicate CFD or steering traces. The distinction between fixed-steer validation and closed-loop application is mandatory for graph and synthesis claims.
