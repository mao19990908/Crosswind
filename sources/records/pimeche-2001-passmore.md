- Car-like bluff body, sinusoidal lateral inflow generated by oscillating aerofoil; not a fixed-yaw-only test.

- Surface pressures integrated for side force and yaw moment; aerodynamic magnification compared against quasi-steady prediction.

## Results / validation / evidence
- Abstract: yaw-moment response can exceed the quasi-steady result by up to 30%; side force is generally lower except at the lowest tested frequency. Scope is this model and input range.
